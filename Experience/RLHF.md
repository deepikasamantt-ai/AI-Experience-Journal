# Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF)

📌 Overview

Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF) is a training methodology used to improve Large Language Models by incorporating human preferences into the learning process. Human evaluators compare, rank, and assess AI-generated responses, enabling models to produce more helpful, accurate, and aligned outputs.

---

## 🎯 Why It Matters

- Aligns AI systems with human expectations.
- Improves response quality and usefulness.
- Reduces undesirable or misleading outputs.
- Plays a critical role in modern LLM development.

- Human judgment remains essential in AI training.
- Consistent evaluations improve model reliability.

- RLHF bridges human expertise with machine learning.
