# LLM Evaluation

📌 Overview

LLM evaluation involves assessing the quality of AI-generated responses against predefined evaluation criteria. The objective is to identify which response better satisfies the prompt while maintaining accuracy, clarity, completeness, and overall quality.

---

🎯 Why It Matters

- Helps improve the quality of Large Language Models.
- Supports model alignment with human expectations.
- Identifies strengths and weaknesses in AI-generated responses.
- Contributes to building more reliable and helpful AI systems.

---

💡 My Experience

During my AI evaluation work, I evaluated AI-generated responses using structured evaluation rubrics.

My work included:

- Evaluating single-response and multi-response outputs.
- Comparing responses generated by different AI models.
- Applying predefined evaluation guidelines and scoring rubrics.
- Rating response quality across multiple evaluation dimensions.
- Identifying strengths, weaknesses, and overall response quality.

---

🧠 Key Learnings

- Consistency is essential during evaluation.
- Small differences in prompts can significantly change model outputs.
- Clear evaluation criteria help reduce subjective bias.
- Attention to detail is critical for high-quality assessments.
