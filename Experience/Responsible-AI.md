# Responsible AI

📌 Overview

Responsible AI refers to the development and use of artificial intelligence in a manner that is ethical, transparent, fair, accountable, and beneficial to society. It emphasizes minimizing bias, protecting user privacy, and ensuring AI systems operate within established policies and guidelines.

---

## 🎯 Why It Matters

- Promotes ethical and trustworthy AI systems.
- Encourages fairness and reduces bias in AI outputs.
- Improves transparency and accountability.
- Builds user confidence through responsible AI practices.

---

## 💡 My Experience

Throughout AI evaluation and annotation projects, I followed project-specific policies and quality guidelines to ensure consistent and responsible assessments of AI-generated responses.

My work included:

- Evaluating outputs against established quality and safety standards.
- Following detailed annotation and evaluation guidelines.
- Identifying biased, harmful, or misleading responses.
- Maintaining consistency and objectivity during evaluations.
- Supporting AI systems that align more closely with human expectations.

---

## 🧠 Key Learnings

- Responsible AI requires both technical and human oversight.
- Fair and unbiased evaluations improve AI reliability.
- Clear guidelines promote consistency across evaluators.
- Ethical considerations are essential throughout the AI development lifecycle.
