# AI Safety

📌 Overview

AI Safety focuses on ensuring that AI systems generate outputs that are safe, reliable, ethical, and aligned with human values. It involves identifying harmful, biased, or misleading responses and promoting responsible AI behavior through evaluation and human oversight.

---

## 🎯 Why It Matters

- Reduces harmful or unsafe AI outputs.
- Encourages responsible AI development.
- Improves user trust in AI systems.
- Supports the development of fair and reliable models.

---

## 💡 My Experience

During AI evaluation projects, I assessed AI-generated responses for safety, appropriateness, and adherence to evaluation guidelines.

My work included:

- Identifying unsafe or inappropriate responses.
- Evaluating responses for policy and guideline compliance.
- Recognizing biased, misleading, or low-quality outputs.
- Applying safety-focused evaluation criteria.
- Supporting responsible AI development through structured feedback.

---

## 🧠 Key Learnings

- AI safety extends beyond technical accuracy.
- Human reviewers play a vital role in identifying potential risks.
- Consistent safety evaluations improve model reliability.
- Responsible AI requires continuous monitoring and feedback.
