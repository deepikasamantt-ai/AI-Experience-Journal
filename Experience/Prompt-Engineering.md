# Prompt Engineering

📌 Overview

Prompt Engineering is the process of designing, refining, and optimizing prompts to guide Large Language Models (LLMs) toward generating accurate, relevant, and high-quality responses. Well-crafted prompts significantly influence the quality and consistency of AI outputs.

---

## 🎯 Why It Matters

- Improves response quality and relevance.
- Reduces ambiguity in AI-generated outputs.
- Enhances consistency across AI interactions.
- Enables more efficient use of LLM capabilities.

---

## 💡 My Experience

While working on AI evaluation and annotation projects, I regularly analyzed prompts and their impact on model responses.

My work involved:

- Reviewing prompts alongside AI-generated outputs.
- Identifying how prompt wording influenced response quality.
- Evaluating responses generated from different prompt variations.
- Understanding the relationship between prompts, instructions, and model behavior.
- Applying prompt-related evaluation guidelines during AI assessment tasks.

---

## 🧠 Key Learnings

- Small prompt changes can produce significantly different outputs.
- Clear and specific instructions improve AI performance.
- Prompt structure influences accuracy, completeness, and tone.
- Effective prompting is an iterative process.
