# AI Annotation

AI Annotation involves reviewing, labeling, and evaluating data that helps train and improve AI models. Depending on the project, this may include text, images, audio, or AI-generated responses.

---

## 💡 Typical Activities

| Activity | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| Data Annotation | Label training data according to guidelines. |
| Response Evaluation | Assess AI-generated responses for quality. |
| Ranking Tasks | Compare multiple responses and rank them. |
| Guideline Adherence | Follow project-specific annotation rules. |
| Quality Assurance | Maintain consistency and accuracy. |

---

## 🧠 My Learnings

- Precise annotations improve AI model performance.
- Consistency is as important as accuracy.
- Complex guidelines require careful interpretation.
- Human feedback plays a key role in AI development.
