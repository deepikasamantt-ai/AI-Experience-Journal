# AI Experience Journal

Welcome to my AI Experience Journal—a living repository documenting my transition from Digital Marketing and Marketing Automation to Artificial Intelligence.

This repository captures my hands-on project experiences, technical learnings, research notes, and continuous exploration of topics such as LLMs, RLHF, AI Evaluation, Prompt Engineering, AI Safety, and Responsible AI.

The goal is to build a well-organized knowledge base that reflects both my practical experience and ongoing learning journey in AI.

## About This Repository

This repository serves as a centralized journal of my AI learning journey and professional growth. It combines project experiences, technical notes, concept explanations, and ongoing learnings into a structured knowledge base.

Rather than focusing only on code, this repository emphasizes understanding, documentation, critical thinking, and continuous learning—skills that are equally important in AI research, model evaluation, and technical communication.
