# AI Evaluation

## 📌 Overview

AI evaluation is the process of assessing the quality, accuracy, safety, and usefulness of AI-generated outputs using predefined evaluation criteria.

---

## 🎯 Why It Matters

- Measures model performance.
- Identifies strengths and weaknesses.
- Supports continuous model improvement.
- Helps build reliable and trustworthy AI systems.

---

## 💡 Evaluation Criteria

| Criterion | What it Measures |
|----------|------------------|
| Accuracy | Is the response factually correct? |
| Completeness | Does it answer the prompt fully? |
| Clarity | Is the response easy to understand? |
| Relevance | Does it address the user's request? |
| Safety | Does it avoid harmful or misleading content? |

---

## 🧠 My Learnings

- Consistent evaluation requires clear rubrics.
- Small differences between responses can significantly affect quality.
- Objective evaluation is essential for improving AI models.
