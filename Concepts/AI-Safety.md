# AI Safety

## 📌 Overview

AI Safety focuses on ensuring that AI systems behave reliably, securely, and in alignment with human values while minimizing harmful or unintended outcomes.

---

## 🎯 Why It Matters

- Reduces the risk of harmful AI behavior.
- Improves reliability and trustworthiness.
- Supports safe deployment of AI systems.
- Encourages responsible innovation.

---

## 💡 Key Concepts

| Concept | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Alignment | Ensuring AI follows human intent. |
| Risk Mitigation | Identifying and reducing potential harms. |
| Robustness | Maintaining reliable performance under different conditions. |
| Human Oversight | Keeping humans involved in critical decisions. |

---

## 🧠 My Learnings

- AI safety is an essential aspect of modern AI development.
- Safety considerations should be integrated throughout the AI lifecycle.
- Human evaluation plays a vital role in identifying unsafe outputs.
