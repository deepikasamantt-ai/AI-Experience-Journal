# Responsible AI

## 📌 Overview

Responsible AI focuses on developing and using artificial intelligence in a way that is fair, transparent, safe, accountable, and beneficial to society.

---

## 🎯 Why It Matters

- Builds user trust.
- Reduces bias and unfair outcomes.
- Encourages ethical AI development.
- Promotes transparency and accountability.

---

## 💡 Core Principles

| Principle | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Fairness | Treat users equitably and minimize bias. |
| Transparency | Make AI decisions understandable where possible. |
| Privacy | Protect user data and confidentiality. |
| Safety | Reduce harmful or misleading outputs. |
| Accountability | Ensure human oversight and responsibility. |

---

## 🧠 My Learnings

- Responsible AI extends beyond model performance.
- Human judgment plays an important role in AI evaluation.
- Ethical considerations are essential when assessing AI-generated content.
