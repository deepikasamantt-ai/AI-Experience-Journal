# Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF)

## 📌 Overview

Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF) is a technique used to improve Large Language Models (LLMs) by incorporating human preferences into the training process. Instead of relying only on training data, human evaluators compare and rate model responses to help align AI outputs with user expectations.

---

## 🎯 Why It Matters

- Improves response quality and helpfulness.
- Aligns AI behavior with human preferences.
- Reduces harmful or low-quality outputs.
- Plays a key role in developing reliable AI systems.

---

## 💡 Key Concepts

| Concept | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Human Feedback | People evaluate AI-generated responses. |
| Preference Ranking | Multiple responses are compared to identify the better one. |
| Reward Model | Learns human preferences from evaluation data. |
| Policy Optimization | Uses feedback to improve future model responses. |
