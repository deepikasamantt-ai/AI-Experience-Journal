# Prompt Engineering

## 📌 Overview

Prompt Engineering is the practice of designing clear and effective prompts to guide Large Language Models (LLMs) toward producing accurate, relevant, and useful responses.

---

## 🎯 Why It Matters

- Improves response quality.
- Reduces ambiguity.
- Enhances consistency.
- Helps solve complex tasks more effectively.

---

## 💡 Common Techniques

| Technique | Purpose |
|-----------|---------|
| Clear Instructions | Define the task explicitly. |
| Role Prompting | Assign a specific role to the model. |
| Context Setting | Provide relevant background information. |
| Step-by-Step Prompting | Encourage structured reasoning. |
| Output Constraints | Specify the desired response format. |

---

## 🧠 My Learnings

- Well-structured prompts significantly improve output quality.
- Providing context reduces misunderstandings.
- Small prompt changes can lead to different responses.
- Iterative refinement is key to effective prompting.
