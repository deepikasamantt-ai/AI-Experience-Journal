# Hallucination Detection

## 📌 Overview

Hallucination in AI refers to situations where a Large Language Model (LLM) generates information that appears convincing but is inaccurate, fabricated, or unsupported by reliable sources.

---

## 🎯 Why It Matters

- Improves the reliability of AI systems.
- Reduces the spread of misinformation.
- Encourages fact-based responses.
- Builds user trust in AI applications.

---

## 💡 Common Indicators

| Indicator | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Fabricated Facts | Information presented without factual basis. |
| Fake References | Non-existent sources or citations. |
| Incorrect Reasoning | Conclusions unsupported by evidence. |
| Confident Tone | Incorrect answers presented with high confidence. |

---

## 🧠 My Learnings

- Hallucinations can occur even when responses sound convincing.
- Verification is essential when evaluating AI-generated content.
- Reliable evaluation requires checking factual accuracy, not just fluency.
